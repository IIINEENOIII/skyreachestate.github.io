Most urgent

Foreclosure / Notice of Sale

A sale date on the calendar changes everything. The earlier we talk, the more of your options stay on the table.

Texas foreclosures move fast once the process starts. Our first job is to understand exactly where you stand: the sale date, the payoff or reinstatement amount, and what the property can actually support.

The honest truth: by the time an auction notice arrives, some options are already gone. A call this week can do more than a call next month.
  • Fast as-is purchase — close before the auction date and walk away with a clean break.
  • Creative structure — in some cases, taking over payments or another structure can stop the clock when a cash offer can't.
  • Referral, not a pitch — if we can't help, we'll point you to housing counseling or legal resources instead of wasting your time.

Family matters

Inherited & probate properties

Settling an estate while grieving is hard enough. The house shouldn't make it harder.

Inherited homes often come with deferred maintenance, out-of-town heirs, and family members who disagree on what to do. We buy as-is, which means no one has to fund repairs or manage a renovation from across the country.

  • We work with heirs and estate representatives through probate timelines.
  • Take what has meaning; leave everything else — cleanout is on us.
  • One buyer, one closing, no showings or open houses.

Property condition

Major repairs needed

Roof, foundation, years of deferred maintenance — sell it exactly as it sits.

Traditional buyers walk away from problem properties, and contractors quote numbers that make your head spin. We're investors — we buy homes in rough condition every month and factor the work into our offer honestly.

  • No repairs, no inspections to fail, no renovation loans.

Carrying costs

Vacant property

Every month vacant costs you taxes, insurance, utilities — and risk.

Vacant homes attract break-ins, code violations, and insurance headaches. Meanwhile the carrying costs pile up. A fast, clean sale stops the bleed in weeks, not months.

  • Close quickly and eliminate carrying costs.
  • No need to travel back to manage the property.
  • We handle cleanout, securing, and everything after closing.
