Selling on your timeline.

Different timelines change what's possible. Here's an honest look at what "fast" versus "flexible" actually requires — so you can choose with your eyes open.

Urgent

0–14 days
  1. Day 1: We talk through your deadline and numbers. If there's a path, we schedule a walkthrough immediately.
  2. Days 1–2: Written offer, usually within 24–48 hours of seeing the property.
  3. Days 3–14: Title work runs while your lender prepares payoff figures. We push both sides to beat the clock.

Best for: foreclosure deadlines, urgent relocation, stopping carrying costs.

Start the urgent path

Balanced

2–6 weeks
  1. Week 1: Conversation, walkthrough, and written offer with clear math.
  2. Weeks 2–3: Time to compare our offer against other paths — listing, creative structures, or staying put.
  3. Weeks 3–6: Title, payoff coordination, and move planning at a manageable pace.

Best for: pressure without an auction date yet, comparing options, planning a move.

Start the balanced path

Flexible

1–3 months
  1. Month 1: Full review of every option — cash sale, creative structures, or listing traditionally.
  2. Month 2: If a creative structure fits, time to document it properly and get comfortable.
  3. Month 3: Close on your schedule, with move-out and transition fully planned.

Best for: life transitions that need coordination, maximizing outcome over speed.

What affects your timeline

Four things that speed you up — or slow you down.

Factor 01

Title & lien complexity

Judgments, tax liens, HOA liens, and heirship issues all take time to clear. The cleaner the title, the faster the close. We'll pull title early so there are no surprises at the finish line.

Factor 02

Your lender's payoff process

Reinstatement and payoff figures come from your lender on their schedule, not ours. In urgent cases we start this request on day one — it's often the longest pole in the tent.

Factor 03

Probate & estate coordination

When multiple heirs or a court process are involved, decisions take longer. We structure around it — and we've navigated these timelines before.

Factor 04

Access & move-out timing

We need to see the property to make a real offer, and a closing date has to work for your move. Tell us your constraints upfront and we'll build the schedule around them.
